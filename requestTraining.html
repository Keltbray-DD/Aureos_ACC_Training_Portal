What's covered at each level

Level 1 Training

  • Log in and access your project with ease
  • Navigate ACC modules confidently
  • Find, Share and View Files
  • Upload files using the correct process
  • Manage Project Emails
  • Manage Notifications

Level 2 Training

  • Reviewing files through structured Workflows
  • Using the Mobile App
  • Raising and tracking Issues
  • Creating, responding to, and closing RFIs
  • Using and publishing Mark-ups
  • Adding and managing References

All users that require Level 2 training also need to complete Level 1 first
